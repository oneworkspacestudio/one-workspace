# My Voice preview.11 for WonderPal

Existing managed My Voice app adds a same-PC private bearer route for approved WonderPal read-aloud. An owner grants it from My Voice settings. The app downloads pinned Phonon English Q8 data to private storage, supervises a warm resident runtime and stops it at revocation. There is no new public proxy path, port reservation, capability or package ID. The owner grant and new runtime cease on a protected rollback. Keep model weights and secrets out of release archives.
